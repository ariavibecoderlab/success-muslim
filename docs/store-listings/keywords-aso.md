# ASO & Keyword Strategy — Success Muslim

## Apple App Store keyword field (100 chars max)

Recommended:

```
muslim,islam,prayer,quran,salah,adhan,dhikr,zakat,fasting,ramadan,halal,hijri,qibla,sunnah,iman
```

Notes:
- Apple already indexes the **app name**, **subtitle**, and **developer name**, so do NOT repeat: "Success", "Muslim", "Iman", "Health", "Productivity", "BrainyBunch".
- Use single keywords separated by commas with NO spaces. Spaces waste characters.
- Plurals are auto-handled.

## Google Play (no keyword field — uses full description)

Play indexes the **title**, **short description**, and **full description**. Target each keyword 2–4 times naturally throughout the full description. Already covered in `play-store-listing.md`:

Primary: muslim, islam, prayer times, quran, ramadan, fasting, zakat, salah, dhikr, adhan, sunnah.
Secondary: hijri calendar, qibla, sadaqah, tarawih, qiyam, hajj, umrah, halal lifestyle, productivity, habit tracker, life balance.

## Competitor benchmarks (research before submitting)

| Competitor | Strength to learn from |
|---|---|
| Muslim Pro | Comprehensive prayer + Quran feature set |
| Athan / IslamicFinder | Adhan customisation depth |
| Pillars | Premium minimal aesthetic |
| Ayah | Quran-focused reading experience |
| Almsly | Sadaqah/zakat tracking |

Key differentiation to emphasize in descriptions:
- **Four pillars in one app** (no competitor combines Iman + Health + Wealth + Productivity)
- **Family / class groups** (gamified social accountability)
- **Daily Life Score** (unique balance metric)
- **Refined Islamic Calm aesthetic** (premium design)

## Title formulas to A/B (Play allows custom listings later)

- A: `Success Muslim` (clean brand)
- B: `Success Muslim: Prayer & Quran`
- C: `Success Muslim — Daily Companion`

Start with A; once installs are flowing, run a Custom Store Listing experiment with B vs C in Play Console.

## Subtitle / short description A/B candidates

- iOS subtitle: `Iman, Health & Productivity` (default)
- Alt: `Prayer, Quran & Daily Life Balance`
- Play short desc: `Prayer, Quran, fasting, health & finance — your balanced Muslim life, daily.` (default)
- Alt: `Prayer times, Quran reader, Ramadan, fasting & habits — all in one peaceful app.`
