```
Success Muslim is your all-in-one daily companion for living a balanced Muslim life — bringing together your faith (Iman), health (Sihah), productivity (Amal), and finances (Mal) in one calm, beautiful app.

Built around the four pillars of a flourishing life, Success Muslim helps you stay consistent with worship, take care of your body, get meaningful work done, and manage your wealth with barakah — all guided by Islamic principles.

— THE FOUR PILLARS —

IMAN — Your Spiritual Command Center
• Accurate prayer times for your location (JAKIM + global Aladhan methods)
• Beautiful Adhan notifications you can customize
• Quran reader with Uthmani script, Mushaf mode, and ayah-by-ayah view
• Track Quran reading streaks and surah progress
• Dhikr & Selawat counter with daily targets
• Salah log including Sunnah, Tarawih, Tahajjud, and Witr
• Qada Solat tracker — never lose count of missed prayers
• Sadaqah, Zakat, and Fidyah trackers
• Hajj & Umrah planner, Qiyam planner, Ramadan optimizer
• Daily Dakwah inspiration to share

SIHAH — Wellness & Halal Health
• Intermittent fasting timer with Sunnah fasting protocols
• Daily steps, weight, sleep, and hydration tracking
• BMI insights aligned to your goals
• Calendar heatmaps for fasting and steps

AMAL — Productivity with Purpose
• Daily focused tasks and habit streaks
• Pomodoro-style focus timers
• Life areas balance — work, study, family, deen

MAL — Wealth Tracker with Barakah
• Income, expenses, savings goals, and budget
• Built-in Zakat calculator (Nisab-aware)
• Sadaqah giving log

— FAMILY & COMMUNITY —
• Private family/class groups (WhatsApp-style)
• See your loved ones' worship snapshots
• Encourage each other with leaderboards and activity feed
• Full privacy controls — share only what you choose

— DESIGNED FOR MUSLIMS, BY MUSLIMS —
• Refined Islamic Calm aesthetic — clean, peaceful, mobile-first
• Daily Life Score combining Iman, Wellness, Productivity
• Spiritual gamification with 7-day check-in rewards
• Works offline — your data syncs when you're back online
• 90-day backdate so you can catch up on past days

— PERFECT FOR —
• Anyone wanting to be more consistent with prayer and Quran
• Families who want to grow in deen together
• Students balancing study, ibadah, and self-care
• Working professionals seeking barakah in their day
• Anyone preparing for Ramadan, Hajj, or Umrah
```
