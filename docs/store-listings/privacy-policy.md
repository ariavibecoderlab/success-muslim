# Privacy Policy — Success Muslim

**Effective date:** 1 May 2026
**Last updated:** 1 May 2026

## 1. Who we are

Success Muslim ("we", "us", "our") is a personal Islamic lifestyle and wellness app published by BrainyBunch. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the Success Muslim mobile app and website (the "Service").

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you provide
- **Account data**: email address, password (hashed), display name, optional profile photo.
- **Profile data you set**: location (city or JAKIM zone) for prayer times, timezone, language, gender (optional), date of birth (optional, for age-based content), height/weight (optional, for BMI).
- **Worship and lifestyle logs**: prayers, dhikr counts, Quran reading progress, sunnah fasts, intermittent fasting sessions, sadaqah and zakat records, income/expense entries, tasks, habits, sleep, steps, weight, hydration, dakwah favourites.
- **Family / class group data**: group name, member list, opt-in shared snapshots.
- **Support correspondence**: messages you send to support@successmuslim.app.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically
- **Device data**: device model, operating system, app version, language, timezone, crash logs.
- **Usage data**: feature usage (e.g. "opened Quran reader"), session duration, error events.
- **Approximate location** (only if you grant the OS permission): used solely to compute prayer times. We do not store GPS coordinates on our servers.

### 2.3 Information we do **not** collect
- We do not collect contacts, SMS, call logs, calendar, microphone audio, camera, or precise background location.
- We do not run third-party advertising SDKs.
- We do not sell your data, ever.

## 3. How we use your information

- Provide, maintain, and sync your data across your devices.
- Send local notifications you enable (prayer reminders, fasting timer, habit reminders).
- Show family members the snapshots you opt to share.
- Improve the Service via aggregated analytics (no individual profiling).
- Respond to support requests.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

- **Contract**: to provide the Service you signed up for.
- **Consent**: location and notifications are opt-in via OS prompts.
- **Legitimate interests**: security, debugging, fraud prevention, product improvement.
- **Legal obligation**: tax, regulatory, lawful requests.

## 5. How we share information

We share data only with:
- **Backend infrastructure provider** (Lovable Cloud, powered by Supabase): hosts our database, authentication, and edge functions. Bound by data processing agreement.
- **Sign-in providers** you choose (Google) — only at the moment you sign in.
- **Family / class group members** — only the snapshots you opt to share.
- **Authorities** — when required by law.

We do **not** sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

## 6. International transfers

Data may be processed in regions where our infrastructure provider operates (typically EU/US). We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

## 7. Data retention

- Active account: data retained while you use the Service.
- Deleted account: personal data deleted within 30 days; backups purged within 90 days.
- Anonymous, aggregated analytics may be retained indefinitely.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, PDPA Malaysia, etc.) you may have the right to:
- Access a copy of your data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and data
- Object to or restrict processing
- Data portability (machine-readable export)
- Withdraw consent
- Lodge a complaint with your data protection authority

Exercise any of these by emailing **privacy@successmuslim.app**. You can also delete your account in-app under Settings → Account → Delete account.

## 9. Children

The Service is intended for users **13 years and older** (or higher minimum age where required by law). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact privacy@successmuslim.app and we will delete it.

## 10. Security

We use:
- TLS 1.2+ in transit
- Encryption at rest on the database
- Row-Level Security (RLS) so you can only access your own data
- Hashed passwords (bcrypt/scrypt)
- HIBP (Have I Been Pwned) password breach checking
- Restricted admin roles with audit logging

No system is perfectly secure; we encourage strong, unique passwords.

## 11. Notifications

The app uses **local notifications** for prayer times and reminders. You can disable them in your OS settings. We do **not** send remote push notifications at launch.

## 12. Cookies and similar technologies (web)

The website uses minimal first-party cookies for authentication only. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## 13. Third-party links

The app may link to third-party sites (Aladhan, JAKIM, hadith references). Those sites have their own policies; we are not responsible for them.

## 14. Changes to this policy

We will post material changes here and update the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will be notified in-app.
