# Apple App Privacy ("Nutrition Label") — Success Muslim

Pre-filled answers for App Store Connect → App Privacy.

## Privacy policy URL

`https://successmuslim.app/privacy`

## Data collection summary

The app collects data linked to the user's identity. The app does **not** track users across other companies' apps and websites.

## Categories collected

For each: **Linked to user** = Yes (since tied to account). **Used for tracking** = No.

### Contact Info
- **Email Address** — Linked: Yes — Tracking: No
  - Purposes: App Functionality, Customer Support
- **Name** — Linked: Yes — Tracking: No
  - Purposes: App Functionality

### User Content
- **Other User Content** — Linked: Yes — Tracking: No
  - Purposes: App Functionality
  - Includes: worship logs, dhikr counts, sadaqah/zakat entries, family group messages

### Identifiers
- **User ID** — Linked: Yes — Tracking: No
  - Purposes: App Functionality, Analytics
- **Device ID** — Linked: Yes — Tracking: No
  - Purposes: Analytics, App Functionality

### Health & Fitness
- **Health** — Linked: Yes — Tracking: No
  - Purposes: App Functionality
  - Includes: weight, height, BMI (user-entered only; not from HealthKit at launch)
- **Fitness** — Linked: Yes — Tracking: No
  - Purposes: App Functionality
  - Includes: steps, sleep, hydration (user-entered)

### Financial Info
- **Other Financial Info** — Linked: Yes — Tracking: No
  - Purposes: App Functionality
  - Includes: income, expenses, budget entries (user-entered for personal tracking)

### Location
- **Coarse Location** — Linked: Yes — Tracking: No
  - Purposes: App Functionality (prayer time calculation)
  - Optional — manual zone fallback available

### Usage Data
- **Product Interaction** — Linked: Yes — Tracking: No
  - Purposes: Analytics

### Diagnostics
- **Crash Data** — Linked: Yes — Tracking: No
  - Purposes: App Functionality
- **Performance Data** — Linked: Yes — Tracking: No
  - Purposes: Analytics

## Tracking declaration

- **Used for tracking purposes**: No
- **App Tracking Transparency prompt required**: No

## Data NOT collected

- Sensitive Info, Browsing History, Search History, Contacts, Photos/Videos, Audio Data, Gameplay Content, Customer Support records other than email correspondence, Precise Location, Payment Info, Credit Info, Purchase History.
